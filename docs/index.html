How it works

Speak. Refine. Paste.

One shortcut carries a thought from your voice into the field you already had open. The small paper overlay tells you where it is in the process.

01 · Speak

Start from anywhere.

Press Control + Option + Space and talk naturally. The overlay appears without taking over the app you were using.

Recording…Press the shortcut again to stop
00:12
02 · Refine

Let the ink settle.

Speech recognition turns audio into words, then Clean tidies speech artifacts without a language model, or Smart Refine improves the text with a local model by default.

Refining…Keeping your meaning intact
···
03 · Paste

Carry on writing.

The result returns to the field where you started. A clipboard copy stays ready if an app blocks automatic pasting.

Private by design

Your words stay close.

01
Audio is processed on your Mac.The included speech model does not send recordings to an InkCling server.
02
Refinement works offline.Gemma runs locally. Optional cloud refinement is used only if you choose and configure a provider.
03
Your journal stays on your Mac.Recent transcripts stay in local application storage and can be cleared inside InkCling.
